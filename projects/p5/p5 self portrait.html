My p5 Self Portrait

Hello everyone! This is my self-portrait I created using p5.js. I tried to incorporate some elements that are true to me while also showing some parts of my personality. My crooked smile and a few strange hairs I think display the kind of person I am in a way. My eyes being wide as ever are also intentional, when I get focused sometimes I forget to blink! I hope everyone enjoys this self-portrait, I think this assignment was really fun. Check out the web editor version here. .Or go back home UPDATE:Hello! I have added an updated version of my portrait with animation and interaction. You can see my eyes grow large and return back to their original size just to do it over again. You can also try the interaction aspect I added by pressing down while moving your mouse on the portrait. You should notice that you can now draw!
